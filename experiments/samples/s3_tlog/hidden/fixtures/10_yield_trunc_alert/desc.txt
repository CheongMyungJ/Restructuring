vs 66.67 threshold), 7/11 -> 63.63 (alert vs 63.64), 1/7 -> 14.28, 2/3, 1/3, 5/6, 0/12, 199/200 -> 099.50,

NULL ALERT_YIELD never alerts. BIN2 counts as pass (TB_BIN_CODE.PASS_YN). Report ordering by PROD_CD, LOT_ID.
